Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 29, 2026

New Beginnings DSC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or send us a booking request.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide: When you send a request through our inquiry form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if provided), the stay you're interested in, your requested dates, number of guests, and any message you include.

Information collected automatically: Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit, for security and reliability purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to your booking requests and questions
  • Confirm availability and arrange your stay
  • Communicate with you about your reservation
  • Maintain the security and functionality of our website

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only with the service providers that help us operate this website:

  • Formspree. Processes and delivers our inquiry form submissions.
  • Cloudflare. Hosts and secures our website.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

These providers process data on our behalf in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website uses a small amount of browser storage to remember basic preferences (for example, dismissing the announcement banner during your visit). We do not currently use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
